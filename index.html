About

I am a communications engineering researcher working on wireless communications and sensing, statistical signal processing and estimation theory, and passive radar. I am currently pursuing my PhD studies in Electrical Engineering and Intelligent Systems at the University of Coimbra, Portugal.

Before that, I completed my M.Sc. in Communication and Information Technology at the University of Bremen, Germany, where my work covered 5G positioning, massive MU-MIMO detection on FPGAs, and network simulation. Publication

Accepted, IEEE GLOBECOM 2026

Accuracy of Joint Time-Based and Carrier-Phase Positioning in 5G Networks under Correlated Measurement Errors

N. Islam, M. Razzaghpour, M. Hammouda, C. Bockelmann, A. Dekorsy

We derive the Cramér-Rao lower bound (CRLB) for joint time of arrival (ToA) and carrier-phase positioning when measurement errors are correlated, and compare it with simulated root-mean-square error (RMSE) across transmit power, bandwidth, carrier frequency, and antenna configurations. In factory and urban scenarios, the approach reaches RMSE below 10 cm.
